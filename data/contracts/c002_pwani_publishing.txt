EXCLUSIVE PUBLISHING AGREEMENT

Between PWANI PUBLISHING LIMITED ("the Publisher") and ZAWADI ("the Writer").
Dated: 3 October 2026. Reference: PP/26/044.

1. PARTIES
The Publisher is a music publisher based in Mombasa. The Writer is a songwriter professionally known as "Zawadi".

2. COMPOSITIONS
"Compositions" means every musical work written or co-written by the Writer during the Term, including lyrics and melodies, and the Writer's share of any co-written work.

3. GRANT OF RIGHTS
The Writer assigns to the Publisher, exclusively and throughout the world, the copyright in the Writer's share of the Compositions for the Term and the Retention Period described in clause 10.

4. TERM
The Term of this Agreement is ten (10) years from the date of signature.

5. ADMINISTRATION
The Publisher shall register the Compositions with collecting societies, issue licences and collect income on the Writer's behalf.

6. ADVANCE
The Publisher shall pay the Writer an advance of USD 800 on signature, recoupable from the Writer's royalties.

7. ROYALTIES
The Publisher shall pay the Writer seventy percent (70%) of net publishing income received in respect of the Compositions. Royalties are recoupable against the advance.

8. NEW USES
The Publisher may use and license the Compositions in connection with new and emerging technologies, including machine learning and automated content systems, in such manner as the Publisher reasonably considers appropriate.

9. CROSS-COLLATERALISATION
Unrecouped advances under this Agreement may be recouped from income under any other agreement between the Publisher and the Writer.

10. REVERSION
On expiry of the Term the Publisher may continue to collect income for a Retention Period of two (2) years, after which all rights in the Compositions revert to the Writer.

11. STATEMENTS
The Publisher shall send statements every six months. The Writer may request a copy of a statement but has no right to inspect or audit the Publisher's books.

12. SYNCHRONISATION
The Publisher shall not grant a synchronisation licence for any Composition without the Writer's prior written approval.

13. CO-WRITER SPLITS
Where a Composition is co-written, the Writer's share shall be as set out in the split sheet signed by all co-writers. The shares in each split sheet shall total one hundred percent (100%).

14. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya.
