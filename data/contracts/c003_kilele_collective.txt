NON-EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION LICENCE

Between KILELE COLLECTIVE ("the Collective") and ZAWADI ("the Artist").
Dated: 5 October 2026. Reference: KC-0912.

1. PARTIES
The Collective is an artist-run distribution cooperative based in Nairobi. The Artist is a singer-songwriter professionally known as "Zawadi".

2. RECORDINGS
"Recordings" means the master recordings the Artist chooses to deliver to the Collective, listed in Schedule A.

3. LICENCE
The Artist grants the Collective a non-exclusive licence to distribute the Recordings to digital services in East Africa for the Term. The Artist keeps ownership of the Recordings at all times.

4. TERM
The Term of this licence is five (5) years from the date of signature.

5. REVENUE SHARE
The Collective shall pay the Artist seventy percent (70%) of net receipts. No advance is paid and nothing is recoupable.

6. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
The Collective shall not use, or permit any third party to use, the Recordings or the Artist's voice to train or develop artificial intelligence systems, unless the Artist gives separate written opt-in consent for a specific use, for a fee agreed in writing.

7. NAME AND LIKENESS
The Collective may use the Artist's name and approved photographs only to promote the Recordings. No synthetic or digitally generated reproduction of the Artist's voice or likeness is permitted.

8. REVERSION
On expiry or termination of the Term, all rights granted under this licence end automatically and the Collective shall take the Recordings down within thirty days.

9. AUDIT
The Artist may, once per year, inspect the Collective's books relating to the Recordings.

10. SYNCHRONISATION
The Collective shall not license any Recording for synchronisation without the Artist's prior written approval for each use.

11. CO-WRITERS
The Collective shall pay any co-writer shares as later notified by the Artist.

12. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this licence on sixty days' written notice.

13. GOVERNING LAW
This licence is governed by the laws of Kenya.
