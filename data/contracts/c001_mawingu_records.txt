EXCLUSIVE RECORDING AGREEMENT

Between MAWINGU RECORDS LIMITED ("the Label") and ZAWADI ("the Artist").
Dated: 1 October 2026. Reference: MR/2026/017.

1. PARTIES
The Label is a record company incorporated in Nairobi. The Artist is a singer-songwriter professionally known as "Zawadi".

2. DEFINITIONS
"Masters" means every sound recording embodying the Artist's performances made during the Term, including demos, alternate takes and live recordings. "Album" means a set of not fewer than ten Masters.

3. ENGAGEMENT
The Artist engages exclusively with the Label to record and deliver one Album during the first contract period, and further Albums if the Label exercises its options under clause 18.

4. GRANT OF RIGHTS
The Artist assigns to the Label all right, title and interest in and to the Masters, throughout the universe, in perpetuity, exclusively, including all renewals and extensions of copyright.

5. DURATION OF RIGHTS
The rights granted under clause 4 shall continue in perpetuity and shall survive any expiry or termination of this Agreement.

6. ADVANCE
The Label shall pay the Artist a non-returnable advance of USD 2,000, payable on signature. The advance is fully recoupable from all royalties payable under this Agreement.

7. NEW TECHNOLOGIES AND DATA
The Artist grants the Label the right to use the Masters, the Artist's performances and any associated data to train, test, fine-tune or improve artificial intelligence and machine learning systems, whether owned by the Label or by third parties, without further notice, approval or payment to the Artist.

8. DELIVERY
The Artist shall deliver technically satisfactory Masters within nine (9) months of signature. The Label decides in its sole discretion whether a Master is commercially satisfactory.

9. NAME, VOICE AND LIKENESS
The Artist grants the Label the right to use the Artist's name, image, likeness, biography and voice, including any synthetic or digitally generated reproduction of the Artist's voice, for any purpose connected with the Label's business.

10. MARKETING
The Label shall use reasonable efforts to market the Album in Kenya. Marketing costs above USD 500 are recoupable.

11. ROYALTIES
The Label shall pay the Artist a royalty of twelve percent (12%) of net receipts from exploitation of the Masters. All royalties are recoupable against the advance and all recoupable costs.

12. ACCOUNTING AND AUDIT
The Label shall render statements twice a year. The Artist may, once per year and on thirty days' notice, appoint an accountant to inspect the Label's books relating to the Masters.

13. REVERSION
The rights in the Masters shall not revert to the Artist in any circumstances, including where the Masters are not commercially released.

14. TERMINATION
The Label may terminate this Agreement on thirty days' written notice. The Artist may terminate only for the Label's material breach not remedied within ninety days.

15. CROSS-COLLATERALISATION
The Label may recoup any unrecouped advance or cost under this Agreement, or under any other agreement between the Label and the Artist, from royalties earned under this Agreement or any other such agreement.

16. SYNCHRONISATION
The Label may license the Masters for use in films, adverts and games. Uses in political or religious adverts require the Artist's prior written approval.

17. WARRANTIES
The Artist warrants that the Masters are original and do not infringe the rights of any third party.

18. OPTIONS
The Label has three (3) consecutive options, each exercisable by the Label alone, to extend this Agreement for a further contract period and require a further Album on the same terms.

19. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya.
